## 1. Company Overview

**DOM Transsport UK LTD** is a specialist service provider that uniquely bridges the gap between **Transport & Logistics** and **Property Services**. Based in Birmingham with nationwide coverage, the company stands out by offering a "fusion" of services designed to support landlords, estate agents, logistics networks, and private clients under one professional roof.

The company prides itself on **structured operations**, **reliability**, and **professional presentation**. Unlike standard "man and van" services, DOM Transsport operates with uniformed, DBS-checked professionals, detailed route planning, and high-level corporate standards.
